Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Mixby is an independent student community app launching at York University, with plans to expand to more campuses. It is a student project and is not run by or affiliated with York University. This policy explains what information Mixby collects, how it is used, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information: your York email address, a display name (it starts as the name part of your York email and you can change it), an optional bio, the degree program you choose, confirmation that you are 18 or older, and a hashed password if you set one. If you sign in with Google, we receive your York Google account's email address and name from Google to confirm who you are.
  • What you post: posts in course forums and the General Forum (with up to 6 photos) and comments (a comment can include one photo, for example one picked from your notes), messages in project groups (including photos and PDF files you send), emoji reactions, reports you submit, and messages you send the Mixby team from Profile → Contact Mixby (with which app and screen size you used, to help us fix bugs).
  • Course and group activity: the courses you join or create, the project groups you belong to, and when you last read a chat (for unread counts).
  • Device and app data: a sign-in session token, and if you allow notifications in the iPhone app, a push notification token for your device.
  • Feed activity: the Feed posts you have already seen (so new posts are shown to you first) and your vote on two-option polls. Classmates see poll totals and like counts, never who voted or who saw a post. These records are deleted with the post or your account.
  • Search taps: when you search a course chat and tap a result, we record that you tapped that message for that search (once per person), so useful answers show higher for your classmates. Classmates only ever see the ranking, never who tapped what. These records are deleted with the message, the course chat or your account.
  • Basic usage events: simple records of actions such as creating a course, post or group, linked to your account and kept in Mixby's own database. They are used to keep the app working and to understand which features are used, and are not shared with anyone.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. Mixby does not use third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

How we use it

  • To run Mixby: show your posts and messages to the people they are meant for, keep you signed in, and send the verification codes and notifications you asked for.
  • To keep Mixby safe during its York launch: verify York emails, review reports, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. Mixby shows no ads.

Who can see your information

  • Other students: your display name, bio and degree, and what you post in course forums, the General Forum and project groups, are visible to the people in those spaces. Private (invite-only) project groups are visible only to their members. Visitors browsing without an account can read posts and chats and see display names, but not other profile details.
  • Your course notes: notes you write under a course (text and photos) are private: only you can see them, unless you choose to share one in a course forum. They are saved on your device first and then synced to Mixby, so you can open them on your other devices. They are deleted when you delete the note, the entry or your account.
  • People browsing as guests: anyone can open Mixby without an account and read one post in each course forum; every other post and comment is hidden until they sign up. The author of that post is shown to them as "York Student", and they can't see member names, comment, post or create anything. They can report that post. Mixby Team posts stay labelled.
  • Your York email is never shown to other students. Only you can see it, on your own profile.
  • People you block no longer appear to you, can't add you to project groups, and don't send you notifications.
  • Moderators: Mixby moderators can see reported content, a copy of it saved with the report, and the reporting details needed to review it.
  • Service providers that run Mixby on our behalf: Supabase (database and file storage), Render (servers), Vercel (website hosting), Resend (verification emails), Apple (iPhone push notifications) and Google (sign-in with Google, and the fonts the website and app load). They only process data to provide their service to Mixby. Some of them store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States.
  • We may share information if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Notifications

Mixby shows notifications inside the app. If you allow it, the iPhone app also sends push notifications through Apple's push notification service for comments on your posts, replies to your comments, project group invites, and new messages in your project groups. Notifications are never sent to you from people you have blocked.

How long we keep your data

We keep your account and what you post while your account exists, except as described below. These limits are applied automatically once a day.

  • Project groups close after 30 days without a new message. A closed group, with its messages and files, is permanently deleted 6 months after it closes.
  • Course chats close at the end of each term. Their messages and files are permanently deleted 12 months after the chat closes.
  • Course forums reset at the end of each term (Fall, Winter or Summer; full-year courses after their second term). Their posts and comments stop being shown, and are permanently deleted once they are 12 months old.
  • General Forum posts and comments stay until you, a moderator, or account deletion removes them.
  • Messages to the Mixby team (Contact Mixby) are read only by the Mixby team, kept until we have dealt with them, and deleted 12 months after that, or with your account.
  • Reports: a report that is still open is kept, with the content it is about, until a moderator resolves it. A resolved report is deleted 12 months after it is resolved. Content a moderator removed is deleted once it is 12 months old, unless an open report still needs it.
  • Uploaded photos and files are deleted together with the message, post, group or account they belong to. Uploads that were never sent, or that nothing uses any more, are deleted within about a day.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own posts, comments and course chat messages at any time, and any photos or files attached to them are deleted too. You can delete your whole account in the app under Profile → Account & Security → Delete account. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone, and you are signed out everywhere. It permanently removes your profile, your posts, comments, messages and reactions, your memberships, and the photos and files you uploaded. Project groups you created are deleted with your account, including other members' messages in them, and replies to your comments are removed with them. Reports you filed are deleted; reports about your content are kept, without a link to your account, until they reach the retention limit above. You can also email us to delete your account.

Age

Mixby currently supports York University students who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
