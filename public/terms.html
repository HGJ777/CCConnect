9. Academic Communities and Group Lifecycle

Mixby provides academic communities that may include course forums, a General Forum, and optional project or study groups.

Course Forums and the General Forum

Course forums let students in a course post and comment. The General Forum is for discussion that isn't tied to one course. Visitors can read one post in each course forum, with the author's name hidden. Everything else is hidden until they sign up.

Course forums reset at the end of their academic term (Fall, Winter or Summer; full-year courses reset after their second term). When a course forum resets, its members are removed and its posts and comments are no longer shown. They are scheduled for deletion once they are 12 months old, subject to exceptions described in the Privacy Policy. General Forum posts do not reset.

Posts and comments may remain available according to Mixby's applicable content and account retention rules.

Earlier Course Chats

Mixby previously offered course chats, which have been replaced by course forums.

Closed course chat messages and associated uploaded files are scheduled for deletion 12 months after closure, subject to exceptions described in the Privacy Policy.

Project and Study Groups

Optional project or study groups may close automatically after 30 consecutive days without qualifying message activity.

Group closure makes the group unavailable through the normal application interface.

Messages and associated uploaded files belonging to closed project groups are scheduled for deletion 6 months after closure, subject to exceptions described in the Privacy Policy.

Mixby may provide advance notice before an inactivity-based closure. A qualifying new message may reset the inactivity period.

Group creators may also have the ability to delete groups directly.

Content Availability

Users should not rely on Mixby as the sole permanent storage location for important academic information.

We may change group lifecycle rules with appropriate notice.

Detailed information about message retention, file deletion, and moderation-related exceptions is provided in our Privacy Policy.
